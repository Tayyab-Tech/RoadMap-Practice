1. What is a business question?
A business question is a question a company wants to answer to make a better decision.
For example, imagine you own an e-commerce store.

Vague business problem
"Our sales are bad. What is happening?"
This doesn't specify what is wrong, how to measure it, or what period to investigate.



Measurable business question
"Why did monthly revenue decrease by 15% compared with the previous month, and which products or regions contributed most to the decline?"
This identifies a metric, comparison period, and areas to investigate.



The second question gives a data analyst a clear direction for investigation.
