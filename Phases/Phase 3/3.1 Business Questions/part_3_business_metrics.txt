Business goal	            Measurable metric	    Example question

Increase sales	            Revenue	                Which products generate the most revenue?
Improve profitability	    Profit margin	        Which products have the lowest profit margins?
Retain customers	        Churn rate	            What percentage of customers stop purchasing?
Improve marketing	        Conversion rate	        Which campaign generates the most purchases per visitor?
Improve operations	        Delivery time	        Which delivery regions have the longest delays?
Increase customer value	    Average order value	    Is the average amount spent per order increasing?



Some useful formulas:

Revenue = Price x Quantity Sold

Conversion Rate = (Purchases / Visitors) x 100

Profit Margin = (Revenue - Costs / Revenue) x 100

Churn Rate = (Customers Lost / Customers at Start) x 100


Always define the denominator and time period consistently when calculating a rate.
