The five steps for translating business problems:

Step 1 — Identify the business goal
What does the business want to improve?
Examples: increase revenue, reduce costs, improve customer retention, or increase profit.



Step 2 — Choose measurable metrics
Decide how success or failure will be measured.
Examples: revenue, conversion rate, profit margin, customer churn rate, or average order value.



Step 3 — Define the scope
Specify the time period, customers, products, regions, or business segments.
Example: online sales in Lahore during the last three months.



Step 4 — Define the analysis
Decide what comparisons or investigations could answer the question.
Example: compare revenue by product category and month.



Step 5 — Define the decision
What action might the business take based on the findings?
Example: improve the availability of popular products or investigate declining categories.
