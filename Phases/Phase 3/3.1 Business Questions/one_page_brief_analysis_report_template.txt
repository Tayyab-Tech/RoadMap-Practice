===========  Example : 


1. Business problem :

Monthly revenue appears to be declining, and management wants to understand why.

2. Primary business question : 

How has revenue changed over the last six months, and which product categories contribute most to the change?

3. Business objective : 

Identify the major contributors to declining revenue and recommend actions to improve sales.

4. Key metrics : 

- Total revenue
- Number of orders
- Average order value (AOV)
- Revenue by product category
- Monthly revenue growth rate

5. Scope : 

- Period: last six complete months
- Data: orders, order items, products, and order dates
- Segments: product categories and months

6. Analysis plan :

- Compare monthly revenue.
- Identify categories with the largest increases or decreases.
- Check whether changes in order volume or AOV explain the revenue trend.
- Investigate missing data and cancelled orders.

7. Expected deliverable :

A one-page report containing key metrics, visualizations, findings, limitations, and recommendations.

8. Potential business decision :

Prioritize investigation of underperforming categories and develop targeted actions based on the evidence.
