# KPI Dictionary — E-commerce Analytics

## 1. Net Revenue

- **KPI name:** Net Revenue
- **Business definition:** Revenue remaining after discounts and refunds, excluding sales tax.
- **Formula:** Gross Sales - Discounts - Refunds
- **Calculation:** $45,000 - $3,000 - $2,000 = $40,000
- **Required data:** Gross sales, discounts, refunds.
- **Measurement period:** Monthly.
- **Assumptions/exclusions:** Sales tax is excluded. Discounts and refunds are deducted from gross sales.
- **Business use:** Measures revenue available to cover product costs and operating expenses.

## 2. Average Order Value (AOV)

- **KPI name:** Average Order Value
- **Business definition:** Average net revenue generated per completed order.
- **Formula:** Net Revenue / Completed Orders
- **Calculation:** $40,000 / 750 = $53.33
- **Required data:** Net revenue, completed orders.
- **Measurement period:** Monthly.
- **Assumptions/exclusions:** Uses completed orders as the denominator and net revenue after discounts and refunds.
- **Business use:** Evaluates basket value, upselling, cross-selling, and pricing strategies.

## 3. Gross Profit

- **KPI name:** Gross Profit
- **Business definition:** Revenue remaining after deducting the cost of goods sold.
- **Formula:** Net Revenue - COGS
- **Calculation:** $40,000 - $18,000 = $22,000
- **Required data:** Net revenue, COGS.
- **Measurement period:** Monthly.
- **Assumptions/exclusions:** Operating expenses and marketing expenses are not deducted.
- **Business use:** Evaluates product profitability and the ability to cover operating costs.

## 4. Gross Profit Margin

- **KPI name:** Gross Profit Margin
- **Business definition:** Percentage of net revenue remaining after product costs.
- **Formula:** (Gross Profit / Net Revenue) * 100
- **Calculation:** ($22,000 / $40,000) * 100 = 55%
- **Required data:** Net revenue, gross profit or COGS.
- **Measurement period:** Monthly.
- **Assumptions/exclusions:** Based on the same revenue and COGS definitions used for gross profit.
- **Business use:** Supports pricing, product mix, and supplier-cost decisions.

## 5. Website Conversion Rate

- **KPI name:** Website Conversion Rate
- **Business definition:** Percentage of website sessions that result in completed orders.
- **Formula:** (Completed Orders / Website Sessions) * 100
- **Calculation:** (750 / 25,000) * 100 = 3%
- **Required data:** Website sessions, completed orders.
- **Measurement period:** Monthly.
- **Assumptions/exclusions:** Assumes the order and session figures cover the same period. This is a session-based rate, not a unique-visitor conversion rate.
- **Business use:** Evaluates website effectiveness and helps identify opportunities to improve the purchasing journey.

## 6. Cart Abandonment Rate

- **KPI name:** Cart Abandonment Rate
- **Business definition:** Percentage of created shopping carts that do not convert into purchases.
- **Formula:** ((Carts Created - Carts Converted) / Carts Created) * 100
- **Calculation:** ((1,000 - 300) / 1,000) * 100 = 70%
- **Required data:** Shopping carts created, shopping carts converted.
- **Measurement period:** Monthly, using consistently defined cart cohorts.
- **Assumptions/exclusions:** Assumes created and converted carts are measured consistently. Carts converted during a later period may require cohort tracking.
- **Business use:** Identifies checkout friction, unexpected costs, payment problems, and other barriers to purchase.

## 7. Customer Retention Rate

- **KPI name:** Customer Retention Rate
- **Business definition:** Percentage of the starting customer base that remains a customer by the end of the period.
- **Formula:** ((Ending Customers - New Customers) / Starting Customers) * 100
- **Calculation:** ((2,200 - 600) / 2,000) * 100 = 80%
- **Required data:** Starting customers, ending customers, new customers.
- **Measurement period:** Monthly.
- **Assumptions/exclusions:** Assumes ending customers include retained and newly acquired customers, with no other adjustments to the customer count.
- **Business use:** Measures customer retention and helps evaluate loyalty and customer relationship strategies.

## 8. Repeat Purchase Rate

- **KPI name:** Repeat Purchase Rate
- **Business definition:** Percentage of unique purchasing customers who made at least two purchases during the measurement period.
- **Formula:** (Customers with 2+ Purchases / Total Unique Purchasing Customers) * 100
- **Calculation:** (250 / 600) * 100 = 41.67%
- **Required data:** Customers with 2+ purchases, total unique purchasing customers.
- **Measurement period:** Monthly.
- **Assumptions/exclusions:** Assumes repeat purchasers are included in the total unique purchasing customer count.
- **Business use:** Evaluates repeat buying behavior and supports loyalty programs and customer engagement strategies.

## 9. Purchase Frequency

- **KPI name:** Purchase Frequency
- **Business definition:** Average number of orders placed per unique purchasing customer.
- **Formula:** Total Orders / Total Unique Purchasing Customers
- **Calculation:** 750 / 600 = 1.25 orders per customer
- **Required data:** Total orders, total unique purchasing customers.
- **Measurement period:** Monthly.
- **Assumptions/exclusions:** Counts orders rather than individual items. Uses unique customers who purchased during the same period.
- **Business use:** Measures purchasing frequency and helps evaluate repeat buying behavior.

## 10. Net Profit

- **KPI name:** Net Profit
- **Business definition:** Revenue remaining after COGS and the specified operating expenses are deducted.
- **Formula:** Net Revenue - COGS - Other Operating Expenses
- **Calculation:** $40,000 - $18,000 - $7,000 = $15,000
- **Required data:** Net revenue, COGS, other operating expenses.
- **Measurement period:** Monthly.
- **Assumptions/exclusions:** Marketing expenses are already included in other operating expenses and must not be deducted twice. This simplified calculation assumes no additional unreported expenses.
- **Business use:** Measures overall profitability under the stated accounting assumptions and supports financial decisions.

## 11. Net Profit Margin

- **KPI name:** Net Profit Margin
- **Business definition:** Percentage of net revenue retained as net profit.
- **Formula:** (Net Profit / Net Revenue) * 100
- **Calculation:** ($15,000 / $40,000) * 100 = 37.5%
- **Required data:** Net profit, net revenue.
- **Measurement period:** Monthly.
- **Assumptions/exclusions:** Uses the project's simplified net profit calculation and excludes any expenses not supplied in the dataset.
- **Business use:** Tracks profitability relative to revenue and supports comparisons across periods.

## 12. Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC)

- **KPI name:** Customer Acquisition Cost
- **Business definition:** Average marketing cost required to acquire one new customer.
- **Formula:** Customer Acquisition Marketing Spend / New Customers Acquired
- **Calculation:** $5,000 / 600 = $8.33 per customer
- **Required data:** Customer acquisition marketing spend, new customers acquired.
- **Measurement period:** Monthly, using consistent marketing attribution and acquisition windows.
- **Assumptions/exclusions:** Assumes all $5,000 in marketing expenses were attributable to acquiring the 600 new customers. Other acquisition costs are excluded.
- **Business use:** Evaluates acquisition efficiency and helps determine whether marketing costs are sustainable relative to customer value.
