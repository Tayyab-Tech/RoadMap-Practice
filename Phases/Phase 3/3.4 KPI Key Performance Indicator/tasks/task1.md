# Task 1 — Revenue KPIs
### Calculate:
- **Net revenue.**
- **Average order value (AOV).**
- **Gross profit.**
- **Gross profit margin.**

For each KPI, write its formula and explain what the result tells the business.







## 1. Net revenue : 

### Formula :
        Net Revenue = Gross Sales - Discounts - Refunds

### Calculation : 
        net_revenue = 45000 - 3000 - 2000
        net_revenue = 40000

### Business Interpretation : 
Store generated $40,000 in revenue after accounting for discounts and refunds.This is the revenue available to cover product costs and operating expenses, excluding sales tax.


## 2. Average order value (AOV) : 

### Formula : 
        aov = Net Revenue / Completed orders

### Calculation : 
        aov = 40000 / 750
        aov = 53.33

### Business Interpretation :
Each completed order generated an average of $53.33 in net revenue. The business could monitor AOV to evaluate product bundles, upselling, and pricing strategies


## 3. Gross profit : 

### Formula : 
        gross_profit = net_revenue - COGS(Cost of Goods Sold)

### Calculation : 
        gross_profit = 40000 - 18000
        gross_profit = 22000

### Business Interpretation :
The store has $22,000 remaining after deducting the cost of the goods sold. This amount must cover operating expenses and leave any net profit

## 4. Gross profit margin :

### Formula :
        gross_margin = (gross_profit / net_revenue) x 100

### Calculation : 
        gross_margin = (22000 / 40000) x 100
        gross_margin = 55%

### Business Interpretation :
For every $100 in net revenue, the store retains $55 after product costs. The remaining 45% covers COGS.
