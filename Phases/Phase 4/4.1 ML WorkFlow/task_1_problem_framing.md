# Task 1 — Problem Framing

## Business Problem

An online shopping company wants to identify customers who are likely to purchase again within the next 30 days. This will help the company improve customer retention and create better marketing campaigns.

## ML Problem Type

**Binary Classification**

Because there are two possible outcomes: Yes or No.

## Target Variable

`will_purchase_again`

- 1 = Customer purchases again within 30 days.
- 0 = Customer does not purchase again within 30 days.

## Five Possible Features

| Feature | Description |
|---|---|
| age | Customer's age |
| total_purchases | Total previous purchases |
| total_spending | Total amount spent |
| website_visits | Number of website visits |
| days_since_last_purchase | Days since last purchase |
