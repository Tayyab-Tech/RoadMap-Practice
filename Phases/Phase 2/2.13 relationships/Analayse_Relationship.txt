How to Analyse a Relationship Correctly

Use this workflow:

Step 1 — Visualize

Create a scatter plot.

Look for:

direction
strength
outliers
clusters
nonlinear patterns

Step 2 — Calculate correlation

Use Pearson correlation when a linear relationship is appropriate.

Step 3 — Fit regression

Estimate the relationship:

 \hat y = b_0 + b_1x 
 
Step 4 — Examine residuals

Ask whether the model is systematically wrong.

Step 5 — Investigate confounders

Ask:

"What other variables could explain this relationship?"

Step 6 — Be careful with language

Instead of:

"X causes Y."

Say:

"X is positively associated with Y."

unless you have appropriate evidence for a causal claim.
